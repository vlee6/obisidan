### Expected value, 2 super-important theorems
- **Linearity**: Given we have a random variables $X$ and $Y$ and constant $c$
	- $E(cX)=cE(X)$
	- $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$, regardless of independence
- **LOTUS**: Given we have random variables $X$ and $Y$ and a function $g$. 
	- Let $Y=g(X)$, then $E(g(X))=\sum\limits_xg(x)P(X=x)$

### Variance
Definiition, given random variable $X$:

$$
\text{Var}(x) = E((X-E(x))^2)
$$

Another way to write it with $E(x) = \mu$:

$$
\text{Var}(x) = E((X-\mu)^2)
$$
One way to calculate it:

$$
\text{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-(E(X))^2
$$

Important facts:
- $\text{Var}(X)>0, \text{unless} P(X=c)=1$
- $\text{Var}(cX)=c^2 \text{Var}(X)$
- $\text{Var}(X+c) = \text{Var}(X)$
- $\text{Var}(X+Y)=\text{Var}(X)+\text{Var}(Y)$ if $X$ and $Y$ are independent

### Negative Binomial Distribution
Fix $r$, the number of successes
$X = \text{\# number of failures before the rth success}$
Support $0,1,2,...$

$$E(X)=\frac{rq}{p}$$

### Poisson Distribution
Poisson regards the counts of a rare event

$$P(X=k)=\frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k}{k!}$$
$$k=0,1,2,3,...$$
$$X \sim \text{Pois}(\lambda)$$

### Indicator Random Variables
$$
I(A) =
\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if } A \text{ occurs},\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
$$
E(I(A))=P(A)
$$
(The Fundamental Bridge) Any probability can be expressed as an expectation problem.
