CDF $F(X)$
PDF $f(X) = F'(X)$

$$
E(X) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x f(x) dx
$$
"Probability is what you get when your integrate density"
#### Uniform Distribution
$$\text{Unif(a,b)}$$
$$
\text{Support} = (a, b)
$$
$$
PDF = 
\begin{cases}
c, \text{if } a < x < b \\
0, \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
Where $c$ is the normalizing constant such that $c = \frac{1}{b-a}$ since ($\int_a^bcdx=1$).

CDF of uniform distribution $X \sim \text{Unif}(a,b)$
$$
CDF = \begin{cases}
0, \text{if } x < a \\
\frac{x-a}{b-a}, \text{if} a < x < b\\
1, \text{if } x > b
\end{cases}
$$
$$
E(X) = \frac{a+b}{2}
$$

### Standard Normal Distribution
$$
PDF = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{z^2}{2}}
$$
$$
\text{Support} = (-\infty, \infty)
$$
$$
CDF = \int_{-\infty}^x \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{t^2}{2}}dt=\Phi(x)
$$
Properties of $\Phi$:
$$\Phi(0)=\frac{1}{2}$$
$$\Phi(-z)=1 - \Phi(z)$$
$$
Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)
$$
$$E(Z) = 0$$
$$Var(Z) = 1$$
### 68-95-99.7% Rule
$$
P(-1 < Z < 1) \approx 0.68
$$
$$
P(-2 < Z < 2) \approx 0.95
$$
$$
P(-3 < Z < 3) \approx 0.997
$$
### Standard Normal
Let $X = \mu + \sigma Z$
$$
X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)
$$
