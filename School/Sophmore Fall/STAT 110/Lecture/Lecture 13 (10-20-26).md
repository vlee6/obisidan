### Exponential Distribution

$$
X \sim \text{Expo}(\lambda)
$$
$$
\text{Support} = (0, \infty)
$$
$$
PDF = 
\lambda e^{-\lambda x}, \text{ if } x > 0 \\
$$
$$
CDF = 
\begin{cases}
1-e^{-\lambda x}, \text{if } x \geq 0 \\
0, \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
$$
E(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda}
$$
$$
\text{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda^2}
$$
Memoryless Property
Imagine I'm waiting for the bus, having waited for s minutes. Any additional time that I'm waiting for the bus results in a fresh random variable with a fresh distribution.

$$
P(X > s + t | X > s) = P(X > t)
$$
### Bus paradox
Blissville vs. Blotchville

Both cities' Buses are 10 minutes apart on average (inter-arrival times).

Blissville times are exactly 10 minutes apart. The average time you need to wait for a bus is 5 minutes.

Blotchville inter-arrivals times are $\text{Expo}(\lambda)$ where $\lambda=\frac{1 \text{ bus}}{\text{10 mins}}$. The average time you need to wait for a bus is 10 minutes by the memoryless property.
