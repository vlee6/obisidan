Let random variable $X$ count the number of **trials** before the first success in an experiment with **independent** trials, each with probability $p$ of success.
$$
X \sim \text{FS}(p)
$$
$$
P(X=x)=pq^{x-1}
$$
$$
\text{Support} = \{1, 2, \dots , n\}
$$
$$
E(X) = \frac{1}{p}
$$
$$
\text{Var}(X) = \frac{q}{p^2}
$$
Note that the difference between this and the [[Geometric Distribution]] is that First Sucess counts trials whereas Geometric counts failures.
