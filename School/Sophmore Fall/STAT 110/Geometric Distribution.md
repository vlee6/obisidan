Let random variable $X$ count the number of **failures** before the first success in an experiment with **independent** trials, each with probability $p$ of success.

$$
X \sim \text{Geom}(p)
$$
$$
P(X=k)=pq^k
$$
$$
\text{Support} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots , n\}
$$
$$
E(X) = \frac{q}{p}
$$
$$
\text{Var}(X) = \frac{q}{p^2}
$$
