$$
E(X) = \sum_{\text{support of }x}x \cdot P(X=x)
$$
### Linearity
Given we have a random variables $X$ and $Y$ and constant $c$
$$
E(cX)=cE(X)
$$
$$
E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y) \text{, regardless of independence}
$$
### Law of the Unconscious Statistician (LOTUS)
Given we have random variables $X$ and $Y$ and a function $g$. Let $Y=g(X)$
$$
E(g(X))=\sum\limits_{\text{support of } x}g(x) \cdot P(X=x)
$$
