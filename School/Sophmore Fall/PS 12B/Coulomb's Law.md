Given two point charges seperated by distance $r$ in meters

```mermaid

```

Coulomb's law gives us a formula to find the electrostatic force exerted on charge 1 by charge 2.

$$
F_{electric} = \frac{1}{4 \pi \epsilon_0} \frac{q_1q_2}{r^2} \hat{r}_{21}
$$

- Let $q_1$ be the charge of charge 1 in coulombs
- Let $q_2$ be the charge of charge 2 in coulombs
- Let $r$ be the distance be the distance between both charges in meters
- Let $\hat{r}$ be the unit vector from charge 2 to charge 1.
