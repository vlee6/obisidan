
$$
\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{Qz}{(R^2+z^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}}
$$
- Let $Q$ be the charge of the ring
- Let $R$ be the radius of the ring
- Let $z$ be the distance of the test point from the center of the ring


Derive from $d\vec{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{dq}{r^2} \hat{r}$

$$
d\vec{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{dq}{r^2} \hat{r}
$$
$$
dq=(Rd\theta)\lambda=(Rd\theta)\frac{Q}{2\pi R}=\frac{Qd\theta}{2\pi}
$$
$$
r = \sqrt{R^2+Z^2}
$$

$$
\hat{r} = \frac{\langle R,\theta, Z \rangle}{\sqrt{R^2 + Z^2}} \text{(in cylindrical coordinates)}
$$
