To be capable of true **double loop** learning, you need to be able introspective and critical of yourself.

- Espoused theory, how you think you act
- Theory in use, how you actually act

Naturally, people, especially those who are successful, don't like to criticize themselves because it makes them feel vulnerable or embarassed. This defensiveness makes it difficult to actually learn because you can't do **double loop** learning.

Initially thought we were supposed to learn something about the pedagogy of this class, but I think what's being described is also a negotiation. Can you balance self-criticism with the criticism of others?

I like most other people get pretty defensive. I can recall times where I'm defending an argument I'm not too sure about just because I don't want the opposing side to "win".
